# AURELIA — Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0  
**Category:** Premium skincare / beauty  
**Positioning:** Science-led skincare with a quiet, refined aesthetic  
**Tagline:** *A little less. A lot more.*

> This is a fictional brand created as a realistic sample for design and marketing workflows.

## 1. Brand foundation

### Mission
Help people build simple, evidence-informed skincare routines without exaggeration, pressure, or unnecessary complexity.

### Vision
A beauty industry where clarity, care, and credible science matter more than hype.

### Values
- **Clarity:** Explain what a product does in accessible language.
- **Care:** Treat skin diversity and individual needs with respect.
- **Integrity:** Make claims only when substantiated.
- **Restraint:** Prioritize fewer, better choices.
- **Inclusivity:** Represent a wide range of skin tones, ages, and textures.

### Audience
- **Primary:** Adults aged 25–45 looking for elevated, straightforward skincare.
- **Secondary:** Skincare enthusiasts who value formulation transparency.
- **Mindset:** Quality-conscious, detail-oriented, skeptical of miracle claims.

### Brand personality
Calm, intelligent, warm, precise, understated.

## 2. Visual identity

### Logo
- Primary logo: uppercase wordmark `AURELIA` with generous letter spacing.
- Secondary mark: minimalist letter `A` monogram.
- Keep clear space around logo equal to the height of the letter `A`.
- Minimum digital width: 120 px for wordmark; 24 px for monogram.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, recolor outside approved palette, or add drop shadows.

### Color palette
| Role | Name | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Porcelain | `#F7F4EF` | Main backgrounds |
| Primary | Ink | `#252A28` | Primary text and logo |
| Secondary | Sage | `#A8B6A5` | Packaging, subtle panels |
| Secondary | Stone | `#D9D2C8` | Borders, dividers |
| Accent | Warm Copper | `#B97958` | Select highlights and details |

**Color rules:** Maintain restrained contrast. Use Ink on Porcelain for long text. Use Copper sparingly, ideally below 10% of the composition. Verify text contrast before publishing; brand colors are not automatically accessible in every pairing.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Cormorant Garamond, Semibold (fallback: Georgia).
- **Body/UI:** Inter, Regular/Medium (fallback: Arial).
- **Headlines:** sentence case, generous line height, no all-caps paragraphs.
- **Body:** short lines, clear hierarchy, high readability.
- Use at most two font families per creative.

### Photography
- Natural soft light; true skin texture; organic materials; clean, quiet spaces.
- Close crops of packaging, hands, botanical materials, or real routines.
- Avoid aggressive retouching, artificial poreless skin, and unrealistic transformations.
- Preferred compositions: open negative space, balanced asymmetry, matte textures.

### Graphic elements
- Thin line dividers and editorial grids.
- Gentle rounded rectangles; avoid busy patterns and glossy gradients.
- Product details and ingredient notes should be treated as informative, not decorative filler.

## 3. Voice and messaging

### Tone of voice
Thoughtful, concise, reassuring without being patronizing. Informative but never clinical for its own sake.

**We sound like:** "A simple routine, thoughtfully made."  
**We do not sound like:** "Erase every flaw overnight!"

### Messaging pillars
1. **Simple rituals:** Make skincare feel manageable.
2. **Formula transparency:** Describe ingredients with context.
3. **Everyday care:** Emphasize consistency over drastic change.

### Copy rules
- Use clear benefit-led headings followed by qualified supporting detail.
- Avoid fear-based language about aging, acne, or appearance.
- Never promise "instant results," "cures," or "100% guaranteed" outcomes.
- Avoid unverified medical or dermatological endorsements.
- Prefer "helps skin feel hydrated" to claims implying disease treatment.

### Sample copy
**Hero headline:** Your routine, refined.  
**Supporting text:** Thoughtfully formulated essentials designed for everyday care.  
**CTA:** Explore the collection

**Social caption:** A few considered steps can make all the difference to how a routine feels. Find the essentials that fit yours.

## 4. Channel guidelines

| Channel | Approach | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Website | Editorial layouts, concise ingredients explanations | Crowded landing pages |
| Instagram | Texture-led imagery, calm educational carousels | Heavy filters, clickbait |
| Email | Warm guidance, one primary action | Aggressive urgency |
| Paid ads | Clear product benefit + visible product | Unproven before/after promises |

## 5. Accessibility and inclusion
- Use sufficient text/background contrast (WCAG AA where applicable).
- Supply descriptive alt text for informative imagery.
- Do not communicate meaning through color alone.
- Include diverse, authentic skin representation.
- Clearly distinguish product features from independently verified results.

## 6. Approval checklist
- [ ] Uses approved color palette and typography.
- [ ] Logo is undistorted and readable.
- [ ] Imagery preserves realistic skin texture.
- [ ] Claims are supported and appropriately qualified.
- [ ] Tone is calm, helpful, and respectful.
- [ ] CTA is clear and not manipulative.
- [ ] Contrast and alt text have been checked.

## 7. Non-negotiables
**Never:** use body-shaming language, fake clinical badges, fabricated testimonials, unsupported efficacy statistics, or misleading photo edits.

**Creative flexibility:** Layouts and storytelling can vary, but scientific integrity and visual restraint must remain consistent.
