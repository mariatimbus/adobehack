# WILDFORM — Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0  
**Category:** Outdoor apparel / adventure lifestyle  
**Positioning:** Durable, versatile gear for everyday exploration  
**Tagline:** *Outside is yours.*

> This is a fictional brand created as a realistic sample for design and marketing workflows.

## 1. Brand foundation

### Mission
Make outdoor exploration feel accessible with practical apparel, useful advice, and a spirit of curiosity.

### Vision
More people confidently spending time outside, from neighborhood parks to mountain trails.

### Values
- **Exploration:** Encourage curiosity, not competition.
- **Durability:** Design for repeated use and repair when possible.
- **Accessibility:** Welcome beginners as well as experienced adventurers.
- **Honesty:** Explain product performance and limits accurately.
- **Respect:** Promote care for public trails and natural spaces.

### Audience
- **Primary:** People aged 18–35 interested in hiking, travel, camping, and outdoor streetwear.
- **Secondary:** Urban commuters who want versatile functional pieces.
- **Mindset:** Independent, experience-driven, design-conscious, value-oriented.

### Brand personality
Adventurous, grounded, energetic, candid, inclusive.

## 2. Visual identity

### Logo
- Primary logo: heavy uppercase `WILDFORM` wordmark.
- Secondary mark: simplified topographic contour shaped like a `W`.
- Preferred logo colors: Forest, Chalk, or Safety Orange on an approved high-contrast ground.
- Keep clear space equal to the cap height of the `W`.
- Minimum digital width: 120 px for wordmark.
- Never place the logo over visually chaotic imagery without a solid or translucent backing that preserves legibility.

### Color palette
| Role | Name | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Forest | `#203B32` | Identity backgrounds, headings |
| Primary | Chalk | `#F2EFE5` | Contrasting backgrounds |
| Secondary | Rust | `#B85F3D` | Lifestyle details |
| Secondary | Stone Grey | `#8A8D85` | Secondary graphics |
| Accent | Safety Orange | `#F46A2B` | Calls to action, wayfinding |

**Color rules:** Use Forest and Chalk as the main system, Safety Orange for intentional highlights. Avoid using Safety Orange as body text over Chalk. Verify accessibility of every color pairing.

### Typography
- **Headlines:** Archivo Black (fallback: Arial Black).
- **Body/UI:** DM Sans, Regular/Medium (fallback: Arial).
- Headlines may be uppercase, punchy, and compact.
- Support with highly readable explanatory copy.
- Avoid elaborate display fonts or fake "distressed" text effects on functional labels.

### Photography
- Documentary style; natural environments; authentic weather and movement.
- Feature different body types, ages, abilities, and skill levels.
- Prefer wide scenes with a strong focal subject and textured details of materials.
- Show gear being used in plausible conditions.
- Avoid dangerous stunts presented without safety context, pristine staged-only imagery, or misleading environmental claims.

### Graphic elements
- Topographic line patterns and route-inspired lines.
- Bold blocks, off-center placement, editorial cut-outs.
- Product spec callouts can use simple pictograms.
- Maintain breathing room around actionable text and product information.

## 3. Voice and messaging

### Tone of voice
Direct, energetic, outdoorsy without gatekeeping. Inviting rather than macho.

**We sound like:** "Take the long way home."  
**We do not sound like:** "Only real adventurers belong here."

### Messaging pillars
1. **Adventure anywhere:** Outdoor moments start close to home.
2. **Gear that goes further:** Practical details and long-term use.
3. **Community outdoors:** Every starting point is welcome.

### Copy rules
- Use active verbs and concrete sensory imagery.
- Talk about real product attributes: material, fit, weather resistance, repairability.
- Avoid unexplained technical jargon.
- Never claim "100% sustainable," "carbon neutral," or "fully waterproof" without appropriate evidence and scope.
- Avoid portraying risky terrain or conditions as universally safe.

### Sample copy
**Hero headline:** GO FIND YOUR OUTSIDE.  
**Supporting text:** Everyday layers made for unexpected turns and familiar trails.  
**CTA:** Discover the gear

**Social caption:** No summit required. Grab a layer, take the scenic route, and see where your afternoon goes.

## 4. Channel guidelines

| Channel | Approach | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Website | Full-bleed outdoor imagery + clear product specs | Vague technical features |
| Instagram | Real stories, route moments, gear details | Exclusivity or gatekeeping |
| TikTok/Reels | Dynamic field footage, useful beginner advice | Reckless challenge content |
| Email | Seasonal utility, field notes, new collections | Constant flash-sale pressure |
| Paid ads | Hero gear + real usage situation | Misleading extreme-performance claims |

## 5. Environmental and safety responsibility
- Specify whether environmental claims refer to a product, component, packaging, or business process.
- Quantified claims need evidence and an identified comparison baseline.
- Encourage Leave No Trace-style behavior without claiming third-party affiliation.
- Include appropriate context when showing high-risk activities or difficult environments.
- Provide alt text and accessible navigation in all digital communications.

## 6. Approval checklist
- [ ] Composition communicates movement, discovery, or utility.
- [ ] Logo and text are legible over imagery.
- [ ] Product specs and performance claims are accurate.
- [ ] Environmental claims are specific and supported.
- [ ] Imagery is inclusive and does not glorify unsafe practices.
- [ ] CTA and key information remain accessible on mobile.
- [ ] Brand feels welcoming to newcomers.

## 7. Non-negotiables
**Never:** invent sustainability credentials, encourage trespassing or unsafe behavior, use deceptive gear-performance claims, or suggest outdoor spaces are only for elite athletes.

**Creative flexibility:** Embrace energetic storytelling, varied photography, and bold layouts while staying factual and welcoming.
